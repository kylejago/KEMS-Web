Public website privacy

The public site does not need access to your live home.

This static kems.uk website is deliberately separated from KEMS property appliances and Home Assistant.

No Home Assistant credentialsThe public site does not request, store or transmit Home Assistant URLs, access tokens or KEMS property credentials.
Only delayed demo evidenceThe optional public demo accepts sanitised daily totals delayed by at least seven days. Precise live power, entity IDs, device identifiers and control surfaces are excluded.
No control pathThe public site cannot call Home Assistant services or issue commands to an inverter, charger, ESP32 panel or other property device.

Future authenticated property access is a separate gateway boundary. It is designed to reach a read-only Pi dashboard through an outbound property tunnel while local Pi management and Home Assistant control remain LAN-only. Hosting providers may retain ordinary web-server/security logs required to deliver and protect the site. KEMS itself adds no analytics or advertising trackers in this release.
