Public website privacy

The public site does not need your home-energy data.

This static kems.uk website is deliberately separated from KEMS property appliances and Home Assistant.

No Home Assistant credentialsThe public site does not request, store or transmit Home Assistant URLs, access tokens or KEMS property credentials.
No household telemetryLive power, tariff, EV, battery, solar and occupancy-adjacent data remain within the property KEMS/Home Assistant environment unless a separate remote-access feature is explicitly introduced later.
No control pathThe public site cannot call Home Assistant services or issue commands to an inverter, charger, ESP32 panel or other property device.

Hosting providers may retain ordinary web-server/security logs required to deliver and protect the site. KEMS itself adds no analytics or advertising trackers in this release.
