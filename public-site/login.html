Private property access

One sign in. Only the KEMS properties you are allowed to see.

KEMS uses Cloudflare Access as the identity boundary. The public KEMS website does not keep its own password database, Home Assistant credentials or property access tokens.

Access path Cloudflare identity → authorised apps → KEMS property

After you authenticate, Cloudflare shows the property applications your account is permitted to access. A different account can see a different set.

How sign in works

Authentication and property permission are separate on purpose.

1

Sign in with Cloudflare

Your identity is checked by the Cloudflare Access policy configured for KEMS.

2

Cloudflare checks access

Each property remains its own protected application. Being signed in does not automatically grant access to every property.

3

Open an authorised property

The launcher presents only what your identity is allowed to open, then routes through that property's outbound tunnel to its read-only KEMS dashboard.
